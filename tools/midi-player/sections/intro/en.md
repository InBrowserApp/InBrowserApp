## Play a MIDI file in your browser

Choose **Open MIDI file** or drop a `.mid` or `.midi` file. **Try an example** loads a short two-track piece. Press **Play** to hear the file with built-in General MIDI instruments. The first playback can take a moment while the instrument sounds load. The player accepts standard MIDI format 0 or 1 with ticks per beat, and playback runs in your browser.

## Seek, change speed, and loop

Drag the **Playback position** slider to jump to a passage, or use its arrow keys to move in small steps. Set **Playback speed** to a slower value when practicing a phrase. The note pitches stay the same. Turn on **Loop playback** to repeat the whole file, or use **Go to beginning** to start over.

## Listen to individual tracks

Use **Mute** next to a track to hear the other tracks on their own, then **Unmute** to bring it back. This works for separate tracks that use the same MIDI channel, too. The track choices and playback speed apply to this session. To save a recording you can share or play in an ordinary audio player, use the [MIDI to WAV Converter](/tools/midi-to-wav-converter/).
