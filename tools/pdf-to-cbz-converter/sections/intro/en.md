## How to convert PDF to CBZ

Choose a `.pdf` comic or drop it onto the converter. Check the cover and page count, then select **Download CBZ**. For example, `volume-01.pdf` becomes `volume-01.cbz`. The conversion runs on your device.

## Original scans and complete pages

A CBZ is a ZIP archive containing the comic pages as images. Each PDF page becomes one image in the archive, in the original page order. When a page contains a single, unmodified JPEG that fills the page, the converter can copy the original JPEG. For other pages, it renders the complete composition so panels, lettering, and visible markings stay on the page. Portrait pages and wide spreads keep their proportions.

## Open the comic archive

Open the downloaded file in the [CBZ Comic Reader](/tools/cbz-reader/) or import it into a comic reading app or library. If a PDF asks for a password, open it with that password and choose an unlocked copy for conversion.
