## How to remove an image background

1. Choose or drop a JPG, PNG, or WebP image. The tool automatically detects the main subject and makes the background transparent.
2. Compare the cutout with the original. The checkerboard shows transparent areas and is only part of the preview.
3. Select **Download PNG** to save the cutout at the original image dimensions. PNG preserves transparency when you place the image over another background.

## Product photos, avatars, and design assets

Remove the backdrop from a product photo to place it over a consistent storefront background. Turn a portrait into a cutout for an avatar or profile graphic, or place an isolated object on a slide, flyer, or thumbnail. The PNG keeps the original canvas size, so it aligns with the source image in a design editor.

## How to get cleaner edges

Start with a sharp image and one clearly defined subject. Good lighting and contrast help the tool separate the subject from its surroundings. Before downloading, inspect hair, fur, glass, and shadows, especially on busy backgrounds or where the subject and background have similar colors.

## Is my image uploaded?

The browser downloads the background removal model and its runtime on demand. The image and the resulting PNG are processed on your device, with no image upload or account required.
