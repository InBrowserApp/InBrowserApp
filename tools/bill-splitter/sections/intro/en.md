## How to split a bill

Enter the full amount you want to share, including tax, tip, or delivery if everyone is sharing those charges. Set the number of people, optionally add names, and choose a split method. Each amount updates as you edit. Use **Copy breakdown** to paste the names and amounts into a group chat.

Use one currency throughout. Enter amounts with up to two decimal places and omit currency symbols and thousands separators.

## Choose a split method

### Equal split

Use this for a group expense everyone shares evenly, like a taxi ride or a set menu. A bill of 100.00 for three people comes to 33.34, 33.33, and 33.33. The shares add up to 100.00 exactly.

### Fixed amounts

Enter a specific amount for anyone who owes a known share. Leave the other amounts blank so those people split the balance evenly. For a bill of 100.00, assigning 20.00 to one person leaves 80.00 for the other two, or 40.00 each. Enter 0 for anyone who owes nothing. When every amount is fixed, their sum must match the bill total.

### By ratio

Use weights when some people should pay more or less than others. For a bill of 100.00, weights of 2, 1, and 1 give shares of 50.00, 25.00, and 25.00. Someone with weight 2 pays twice as much as someone with weight 1 before rounding. A weight of 0 gives that person a zero share.

## How rounding works

The calculator divides the bill in hundredths of a unit. Leftover hundredths go to the largest fractional shares first. Ties follow the order of the people in the list. Fixed amounts stay exactly as entered while the remaining balance is divided.

## Are my bill and names uploaded?

Your entries are calculated in your browser. The tool sends no amounts or names to a server. You decide where to paste the breakdown after copying it.
