## How to convert latitude and longitude

Enter latitude and longitude in decimal degrees or enter the components separately in degrees, minutes and seconds. The other format updates as you edit. Copy the result for a GPS device, a map or a survey note.

## Decimal degrees and DMS

One degree is 60 minutes, and one minute is 60 seconds. To convert DMS to decimal degrees, add the degrees, the minutes divided by 60, and the seconds divided by 3,600. Use a negative value for south or west. Latitude ranges from −90° to 90°, and longitude ranges from −180° to 180°.
