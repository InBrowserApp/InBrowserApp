## How to test your microphone online

Choose **Start microphone**, allow access in your browser, and speak at your normal volume. The input meter should rise as you speak and fall when you pause. Turn on **Hear my microphone** to listen live and check for background noise, distortion, or a muffled voice. Use headphones to avoid feedback from speakers. Choose **Stop microphone** when you finish.

## How to check left and right audio channels

Play **Left**, **Right**, and **Both** to test your headphones or speakers. Left and right should sound only on the matching side. Both should sound centered when the two sides are balanced. The tones play through your current system output and work without microphone access.

## What to check when audio sounds wrong

If the meter stays still, check your physical mute switch and select the correct microphone in your browser or system sound settings. If both left and right tones play on the same side, check mono audio, stereo balance, and whether your headphones are worn or wired correctly. Bluetooth headsets may switch audio modes when the microphone is active. Stop the microphone and repeat the stereo test to compare.

## Does the test record or upload audio?

The meter, live playback, and test tones run in your browser. The tool does not make a recording or upload microphone audio. Live playback can have a short delay that depends on your browser and audio device.
