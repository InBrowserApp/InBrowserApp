## How to calculate the days between two dates

Choose a start date and an end date in **Days between dates**. The result is the number of calendar days elapsed between them. You can enter the dates in either order. Consecutive dates are 1 day apart, and two identical dates are 0 days apart. For example, February 28 to March 1 is 2 days in 2024 and 1 day in 2025 because only 2024 has a February 29. Use this mode to count days until an event or measure how long ago a date was.

## How to add or subtract days from a date

Choose **Add or subtract days**, enter a starting date, and enter a whole number of days. The calculator shows both the date that many days later and the date that many days earlier. The starting date is day 0, so adding 1 day to January 31 gives February 1. Subtracting 1 day from March 1, 2024 gives February 29, 2024. This is handy for deadlines, reminders, and the question “What date is 30 days from today?”

## How to calculate your age from your date of birth

In **Age**, enter your date of birth to see how many full years have passed as of today on your device. The result updates on the birthday, so someone born June 15, 2000 is 24 on June 14, 2025 and 25 on June 15, 2025. For a February 29 birthday, the calculator uses February 28 as the anniversary in years without a leap day.

## Calendar days, time zones, and privacy

Calendar-day calculations include weekends and holidays. They use dates on the Gregorian calendar, so a clock change for daylight saving time does not change the day count. To skip weekends or holidays, use the [Business Days Calculator](../business-days-calculator/). Calculations happen in your browser, and the tool does not upload or save your dates.
