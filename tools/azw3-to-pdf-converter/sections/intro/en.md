## How to convert AZW3 to PDF

Open a `.azw3` file or drop it onto the converter. Check the book preview and choose **A4**, **A5**, or **US Letter**. Select **Save as PDF** and choose the PDF destination in your browser’s print dialog. Turn off headers and footers in the print dialog if you want plain pages.

## What stays in the PDF?

The source text stays searchable and selectable. The printable layout uses the book’s chapter order and keeps supported headings, lists, tables, illustrations and internal links. Page breaks and line wrapping follow the paper size you choose.

## Which AZW3 books work?

AZW3 is Kindle Format 8, also called KF8. Use a compatible, unprotected book with reflowable text. The book is processed on your device. For an older MOBI file, try [MOBI to PDF](../mobi-to-pdf-converter/). To read the original AZW3 book, open it in the [MOBI Reader](../mobi-reader/).
