## Convert a WPS Writer file to PDF

Open or drop a compatible WPS Writer document (`.wps`) or template (`.wpt`). Preview the PDF in your browser and download it when ready. Your file stays on your device.

## Page layout and compatibility

Supported body content is reflowed onto A4 pages and rendered as images at 150 DPI. Encrypted files, older proprietary WPS formats, Microsoft Works files, and files with some complex content are unsupported.

For reading, use the [WPS Viewer](../wps-viewer/). For `.doc` and `.docx` files, use the [Microsoft Word to PDF Converter](../microsoft-word-to-pdf-converter/).
