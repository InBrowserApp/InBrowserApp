## Convert MusicXML or MXL to PNG or SVG

Choose **Open MusicXML file** or drop a `.musicxml`, `.xml`, or compressed `.mxl` score onto the converter. Select PNG or SVG in **Image format**. Choose **Try an example** if you want to preview the workflow with a short piano score. The converter renders and exports the images in your browser.

## Export the whole score, a page, or a few measures

Choose **All pages** to save the full A4 layout. Choose **Current page** and use the arrows in **Image preview** to find the page you want. For a short example on a slide, choose **Measure range**, enter the first and last measures, and select **Preview excerpt**. Count measures in the order they appear in the score, starting at 1 and including any pickup. The converter trims the blank space around the excerpt.

## Choose between PNG and SVG

PNG is a sharp bitmap that you can insert into a document, presentation, or message. SVG keeps the notation sharp at different sizes and is useful on websites or in vector drawing apps. Other apps may substitute the font used for score titles or lyrics in SVG. PNG preserves the appearance rendered by the browser.

## Download the images

Choose **Download image** for a single image. When the export spans several pages, choose **Download ZIP** and extract it to get one numbered image per page. For printing the whole score as one document, use the [MusicXML to PDF Converter](/tools/musicxml-to-pdf-converter/).
