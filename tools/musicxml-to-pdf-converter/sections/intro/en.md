## Convert MusicXML or MXL to PDF

Choose **Open MusicXML file** or drop a score into the converter. It accepts `.musicxml` and `.xml` scores, along with compressed `.mxl` files. Choose **Try an example** to see how a short piano score looks on the page. Use the arrows in **PDF preview** to check longer pieces, then select **Download PDF** to save all the pages as one file. The converter processes your score in your browser.

## Choose A4 or US Letter

A4 is 210 × 297 mm. US Letter is 8.5 × 11 inches and is common in North America. Match **Paper size** to the paper in your printer. Changing it lays out the score again, so check your page turns before downloading. Use the actual-size option in your PDF reader’s print dialog if you want the dimensions shown in the preview.

## Share a print-ready score

The PDF keeps the staff lines and note shapes sharp when you zoom in or print. It is useful for sharing a part with a student, sending a score to a musician, or reading on a tablet. Keep the MusicXML or MXL file if you plan to edit the notes later. The converter lays out your score again for the chosen paper, so spacing and page breaks may differ from the score in your notation program.
