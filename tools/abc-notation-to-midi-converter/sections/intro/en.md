## Convert ABC notation to MIDI

Paste **ABC notation**, or choose **Open ABC file** to open an `.abc` or `.txt` file. **Try an example** loads a short two-tune collection. Check the notes in **Score preview**, then select **Download MIDI** to save a standard `.mid` file that can open in a DAW, sequencer, or MIDI player.

## Listen to a tune before downloading

Select **Listen** to hear the tune shown in **Score preview** with the built-in General MIDI instruments. Use the preview arrows to hear a different tune. The first listen can take a moment while the instrument sounds load. Supported ABC tempo markings, repeat signs, and voices are reflected in the MIDI sequence.

## Export a tune from a collection

An `X:` field starts each tune and `T:` supplies its title. Use **Tunes to include** to choose one or more tunes. One tune downloads directly as MIDI. Choose **Download ZIP** for several tunes to get one numbered MIDI file per tune in their original order, including when tunes share a title.

## Use MIDI for playback and editing

MIDI stores note events, timing, and other playback instructions. Import it into music software to change the instrument sounds or edit the performance. To print the written notation, use the [ABC Notation to PDF Converter](/tools/abc-notation-to-pdf-converter/).
