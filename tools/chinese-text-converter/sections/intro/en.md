## How to convert Simplified and Traditional Chinese

Choose a direction, then paste or type your Chinese text. The result updates as you edit or change the variant. Select **Copy result** to use it in a message, document, or website. The tool keeps paragraph breaks and processes the text in your browser without uploading or saving it.

## Which Traditional Chinese variant should I choose?

- **Standard Traditional** uses OpenCC’s general Traditional forms. For example, `软件` becomes `軟件`.
- **Taiwan characters** uses local character forms such as `裡面` for `里面`.
- **Taiwan characters and vocabulary** also changes common terms. For example, `软件` becomes `軟體` and `鼠标` becomes `滑鼠`. In the reverse direction, it changes those Taiwan terms back to common Simplified Chinese terms.
- **Hong Kong characters** uses local character forms such as `説話` for `说话`.

When converting to Simplified Chinese, choose the variant that matches your source.

## Why does the same character sometimes convert differently?

OpenCC matches words and phrases to choose a character based on context. For example, `发` becomes `髮` in `头发` → `頭髮` and `發` in `发展` → `發展`. Both forms can become `发` again in Simplified Chinese, so a round trip may change the original wording. Check names and specialized terms before publishing.
