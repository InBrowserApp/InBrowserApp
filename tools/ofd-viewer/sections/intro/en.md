## Read an OFD document locally

Open or drop an `.ofd` file to view its pages in this browser. Use the page overview, previous and next controls, or enter a page number. Fit width, fit page, zoom, and rotation help with different page sizes and orientations. Focus reading gives the document more room. Replace or close the file to release the current document.

Files are processed on your device. The viewer does not upload documents, fetch remote document resources, execute document actions, or save a reading history. This is a visual preview; text selection, document search, editing, printing, and export are not available.

## Compatibility and important details

OFD files can contain positioned text, tables, embedded images, and annotations. Their appearance depends on the document features, embedded fonts, and image formats supported by your browser. Substitute fonts can change characters or alignment. Complex graphics, some color effects, and JBIG2 or other unsupported images may be missing or differ from a dedicated OFD reader. Known rendering problems are reported in the reading area; a preview is not a complete format-conformance check.

Digital signatures are not verified. Seal appearances stored inside digital signatures are not displayed; ordinary images or drawings of seals may be visible. A visible seal does not establish authenticity. If a package contains multiple documents, this viewer shows only the first and explains that the package is incomplete here. Check important amounts, missing content, and signature information with an appropriate OFD reader.

There is no fixed file-size or page-count limit. Available browser memory and the complexity of a page can still prevent it from opening. At very high zoom, a page may exceed the browser canvas allocation budget; reduce the zoom or use Fit page.

To create a PDF from supported documents, use the [OFD to PDF Converter](../ofd-to-pdf-converter/).
