## Convert MusicXML or MXL to MIDI

Open a `.musicxml`, `.xml`, or compressed `.mxl` score. Use **Try an example** to load a short score. Browse **Score preview** and choose **Download MIDI** to save a standard `.mid` file for a sequencer, digital audio workstation, or MIDI player. The conversion runs in your browser. When exporting from a notation app, choose partwise MusicXML (`score-partwise`).

## Listen to the converted score

Choose **Listen** to hear the MIDI file through built-in General MIDI instrument sounds. The first listen can take a moment while the sounds load. If the score has several parts, listen for the entrances and timing that matter to your arrangement. You can then change the instruments or edit the performance in your music software.

## Use the right format for your next step

MIDI carries performance information such as notes and timing. The MusicXML score remains useful for editing written notation and preserving the appearance of the page. For a printable score, use the [MusicXML to PDF Converter](/tools/musicxml-to-pdf-converter/). To start from MIDI and get editable notation, use the [MIDI to MusicXML Converter](/tools/midi-to-musicxml-converter/).
