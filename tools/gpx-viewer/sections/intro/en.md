## How to view a GPX file

Open a .gpx file from a watch, cycling computer or route planner. The map shows recorded tracks, planned routes and saved waypoints. Move across the elevation profile, or use its keyboard control, to locate a recorded point on the map.

## How distance and elevation are calculated

Distance is measured along consecutive points in each track segment or route. Ascent and descent use the recorded elevation in meters. Gaps between segments and missing elevation values are preserved. The basemap and calculations work offline after the page loads, and the file stays in your browser.
