## Read a Word document in your browser

Open or drop a Word 97–2003 `.doc` file or a `.docx`, `.docm`, `.dotx`, or `.dotm` file. DOC files open in a continuous reading layout with zoom, selectable text, and an outline when headings are available. The newer formats have page navigation, search, and Markdown export.

## Compatibility and related tools

Documents stay in your browser. Macros and external resources are disabled. Fonts and complex formatting may differ from the original. Encrypted files need an unencrypted copy.

To generate a PDF, use the [Microsoft Word to PDF Converter](../microsoft-word-to-pdf-converter/). For `.wps` documents and `.wpt` templates, use the [WPS Viewer](../wps-viewer/).
