## Browse an Excel workbook in your browser

Open a local .xlsx file or drop it into the viewer. Switch worksheets, scroll through cells, adjust zoom, and inspect cell contents. Common cell formatting and merged cells are displayed where supported.

## Privacy and compatibility

Workbooks are processed locally and are not uploaded or saved by this tool. Fonts come from your device; no online fonts are requested. Formula cells show values saved in the workbook when available. Formulas are not recalculated, and external data connections are not refreshed.

Archive expansion and visible rendering are limited to keep browser memory bounded. Large or complex workbooks may exceed these limits. Encrypted workbooks, legacy .xls files, and macro-enabled files are not supported. This viewer does not edit, export, or print spreadsheets, run macros, or load external document resources. Unsupported charts, images, pivot features, and formatting may differ from desktop Excel or be missing.
