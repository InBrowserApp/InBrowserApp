## How to open a DjVu file online

Choose **Open DjVu** or drop a `.djvu` or `.djv` file from your device. The viewer opens the first page in your browser, with no app or browser extension to install. It works for single-page scans and for multi-page books or documents bundled into one file.

## How to read and zoom

Turn pages with the arrows beside the page number. To jump to a specific page, enter its number and press Enter. When the reading area has keyboard focus, Page Up and Page Down turn pages, and Home and End jump to the beginning or end of the document.

**Fit page** shows the whole scan. **Fit width** makes the most of the available space, which helps when reading small print on a phone. Zoom in further to inspect a diagram or an annotation. **Focus reading** gives the document more room. Press Escape to return to the tool page.

## What is a DjVu file?

DjVu is a document format designed to compress high-resolution scans, such as books, journals, and archived papers. The viewer displays the scanned pages as images, preserving their layout. If a DjVu download contains an index and separate page files, look for a bundled copy with all pages in one file. If one page fails to display, you can still try the other pages.

## Where is my file processed?

The browser reads and renders the document on your device. Opening and reading a file requires no account or upload.
