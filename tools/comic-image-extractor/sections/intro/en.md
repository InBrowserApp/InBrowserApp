## How to extract images from CBZ, CBR, or CB7

Choose a comic file or drop it onto the extractor. Browse the thumbnails and choose the pages you want to save. Choose **Original images** or **JPG**, then use **Download image** for one page or **Download ZIP** for several. The comic and extracted images stay on your device.

## Save original comic pages or convert them to JPG

Use **Original images** to keep the image files from the archive without recompression, including their original resolution and transparency. Use **JPG** if you need a common format for editing or sharing. Converted JPGs retain the page dimensions, fill transparent areas with white, and use the first frame of animated pages.

## Choose specific pages from a comic

Pages follow the natural order of folders and filenames, so `page2.png` comes before `page10.png`. Use **Clear selection** to start with an empty selection or **Select all** to include all available pages. Your selections stay in place as you move between groups of thumbnails. A ZIP download numbers the files in reading order.
