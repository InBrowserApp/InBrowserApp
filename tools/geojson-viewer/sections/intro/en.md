## How to view GeoJSON

Open a .geojson or .json file, paste a GeoJSON object, or load the example. The map fits the data automatically. Pan or zoom, then select a feature on the map or in the list to see its properties.

## GeoJSON geometry and coordinates

The viewer accepts FeatureCollection, Feature and standalone geometry objects, including points, lines, polygons and their multi-part variants. GeoJSON uses WGS 84 coordinates in longitude, latitude order. The bundled basemap shows land outlines and works offline after the page loads. Your files are processed locally in the browser.
