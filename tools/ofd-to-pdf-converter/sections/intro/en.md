## Convert OFD pages to PDF

Open or drop a local OFD file, wait for conversion, inspect the PDF preview, and download the result. Every document in a multi-document package is included in its original order, followed by its pages in their original order. Page sizes and portrait or landscape orientation are retained. Use page navigation, fit, zoom, and focus mode to inspect the converted pages.

## Appearance and compatibility

The PDF contains image pages rendered at 150 DPI, with a white background. Text is not selectable or searchable, and OCR is not added. Links, interactive fields, original vector objects, and document actions are not retained. Missing or unusable fonts may be substituted; a visible notice asks you to check characters, amounts, and alignment before downloading.

This converter does not verify or preserve digital signatures or embedded seal payloads. Files containing them are rejected instead of exporting a document with the seal missing. Ordinary images or drawings of stamps can be included when their graphics are supported; their appearance does not prove authenticity. Missing pages, images, templates, and known unsupported graphics stop conversion, with no partial PDF available for download. Complex OFD features may require a dedicated OFD application.

## Local processing

Files stay in this browser. The converter does not fetch remote document resources or execute document actions. Changing or closing the file cancels the previous conversion and removes its download. Canceling releases the active rendering resources. There is no fixed file-size or page-count quota; available browser memory and canvas allocation limits still apply.

To inspect an OFD file in its original format, open the [OFD Viewer](../ofd-viewer/).
