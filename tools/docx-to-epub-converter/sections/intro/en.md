## How to convert Word DOCX to EPUB

Choose a `.docx` file or drop it onto the converter. Preview the document, then choose **Download EPUB**. For example, `manuscript.docx` becomes `manuscript.epub`. The conversion runs on your device. If your Word document uses the older `.doc` format, save a `.docx` copy in Word first.

## Preserve text, headings, and illustrations

The converter keeps selectable text, headings, lists, tables, links, and supported embedded illustrations. Use Word’s built-in heading styles to create a table of contents readers can use to jump to a chapter or section. The EPUB uses the document title and author when the Word file includes them.

## Read the Word document as an ebook

A reflowable EPUB lets readers change the font size and adapt the text to their screen. Word page numbers, headers, footers, and page breaks are left out. Open the converted file in the [EPUB Reader](/tools/epub-reader/) to check the text and navigation.
