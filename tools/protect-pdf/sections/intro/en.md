## How to password protect a PDF

1. Choose or drop a PDF that opens without a password.
2. Enter the password that recipients should use to open the PDF, then enter it again to confirm. Use the eye icon to check what you typed.
3. Select **Protect PDF** and then **Download protected PDF**. Open the downloaded copy in your PDF reader to check that it asks for your password.

The tool creates a separate file. For example, protecting `invoice.pdf` gives you `invoice-protected.pdf`. Your original PDF stays intact.

## What a PDF opening password protects

A document opening password controls who can view the encrypted PDF. The tool uses AES-256 encryption for the downloaded copy. Once someone opens it with the password, they can print or copy its contents. Restrictions on actions such as printing and editing are a separate PDF setting.

An opening password can be useful when sharing a payslip, a bank statement, a contract, or a completed form. Use a strong, unique password and share it separately from the PDF, such as through a secure message. Keep a copy of the original if you might need access when the password is unavailable.

## Is the PDF uploaded anywhere?

The file and password stay in your browser. Encryption runs on your device, and the protected PDF is saved through your browser’s download menu. You can use the tool without an account.
