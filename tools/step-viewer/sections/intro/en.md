## Open STEP and STP files online

Choose **Open STEP file** or drop a `.step` or `.stp` file into the viewer to inspect a CAD part or assembly in 3D. Choose **Try an example** to explore a mounting bracket before opening your own file. STEP (ISO 10303) is a CAD exchange format. The two extensions refer to the same format. The viewer processes your model on your device without uploading it.

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag or drag with two fingers to pan. The view selector offers isometric, front, top, and right views. Use **Fit to view** to bring the whole model back into frame, or open full screen for more space.

## Check overall dimensions and measure distances

**Overall size** shows the model’s full extent along its X, Y, and Z axes, regardless of how you rotate it. Switch between millimeters and inches to compare the values with a drawing or specification. A 60 mm span, for example, is about 2.36 in.

Choose **Measure** and select two points on the visible model to see the straight-line distance between them. Use a preset view or zoom in to place the points more easily. The viewer measures the rendered surfaces, so the results are approximate, especially on curved geometry. Use the original CAD model to confirm dimensions that affect tolerances or fit.

## Save the current 3D view as a PNG

Set the angle and zoom you want, then choose **Save PNG**. The download captures the model as it appears in the viewer, including a completed measurement and its value. Use it to share a design detail in a review, quote, or assembly note.
