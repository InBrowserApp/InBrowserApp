## How to combine images into one PNG

1. Choose **Add images**, drop in photos, or paste a screenshot from your clipboard. Use the arrows in **Images** to put them in order.
2. Choose a layout and adjust the spacing and background. The live preview shows the final width and height in pixels.
3. Select **Download PNG** to save the combined image. The tool processes your pictures directly in your browser.

## Choose a layout for screenshots or photos

- **Side by side** puts images in one row. Use it for a before-and-after photo or to compare two versions of a screen.
- **Stacked** arranges images vertically, one below another. Use it to make a long screenshot from a sequence of screens. If the captures overlap, [crop the repeated areas](/tools/image-cropper/) first.
- **Grid** arranges images in rows and columns. Set **Columns** to 2 to turn four photos into a 2 × 2 collage.

## Match image sizes without cropping

By default, each image keeps its original dimensions. Turn on **Match sizes** to give side-by-side images the same height or stacked images the same width. In a grid, each image fits inside a cell of the chosen width and height. Images keep their proportions and stay fully visible.

Leave a dimension blank to use the smallest source value for that dimension. For example, to stack two screenshots that are 1,200 and 800 pixels wide, turn on **Match sizes** and leave **Width (px)** blank. Both screenshots will be 800 pixels wide in the result.

## Set spacing and export a transparent background

Set **Spacing (px)** to 0 to remove the gap between images or grid cells. Add a few pixels to separate them. Choose **Transparent** to keep gaps and unused areas see-through in the PNG. Choose **Solid color** to fill that space with a color, such as white for a document.
