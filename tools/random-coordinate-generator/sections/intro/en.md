## Generate random latitude and longitude pairs

Choose **Worldwide** to sample latitude and longitude across the globe, or **Near a point** to generate samples around a center. Enter the quantity and select **Generate**. Copy the batch or download a `UTF-8` text file with one pair per line. Use the coordinates to populate map markers, test location fields, or seed geospatial fixtures.

## Generate coordinates around a point

Enter the center latitude and longitude, then enter the radius in kilometers. For example, enter `37.774900` and `-122.419400` for a center in San Francisco, with a radius of `25` km for nearby points. Latitude ranges from `-90` to `90`, and longitude ranges from `-180` to `180`. A negative latitude is south of the equator, and a negative longitude is west of the prime meridian.

## Read the decimal coordinates

Each line has latitude first and longitude second, separated by a comma. A pair looks like `37.774900, -122.419400`. The output keeps six decimal places and a decimal point in every page language. Keep the order when pasting into another tool, and check the field labels if that tool expects longitude first.
