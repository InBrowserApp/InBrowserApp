## How to test your screen for dead pixels

Clean the screen first so that dust and fingerprints are easier to rule out. Choose **Start fullscreen test**, then inspect the center, edges, and corners on each of the five solid colors. Click or tap the color to advance, or use the arrow keys to go in either direction. Hide the controls to inspect the area beneath them, then tap the screen to bring them back. Use **Exit fullscreen** or Esc when you finish.

## What a dead or stuck pixel looks like

On white, look for tiny dark dots. On black, look for pixels that stay lit. Red, green, and blue help isolate a subpixel that fails to light or stays on for one color. Check whether the dot stays in the same physical spot as you change colors. Surface dust and reflections can look like defects, especially when viewed at an angle.

## Testing a laptop, phone, or external monitor

Use the screen you want to inspect. If you have multiple monitors, exit fullscreen and move the browser to the next display before starting again. On phones, try a different screen orientation for a comfortable view. This is a visual check for pixel defects. For a warranty claim, compare your observations with the display maker’s pixel policy.
