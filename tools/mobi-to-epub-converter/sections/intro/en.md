## Create an EPUB from a local MOBI book

Open an unencrypted MOBI or AZW3 book to create an EPUB you can download and open in a compatible reading application. Supported AZW and PRC book containers are also accepted. Conversion happens in your browser; no account, upload or remote conversion service is involved.

## Keep the reading content together

The EPUB retains available title and author information, chapter order, a contents list, a cover and supported included images. Footnotes and supporting sections remain included, and resolvable internal links point to the corresponding content in the converted book. Empty pagebreak sections are omitted.

Review the book preview and conversion notes before downloading. A missing required chapter stops conversion. Missing or unsupported optional resources are identified, with placeholders for unavailable images. External links open only when selected; remote resources are not fetched.

## Compatibility and layout

This is a reflowable reading copy, not a reproduction of the publisher's original design. Fonts, styles, precise page breaks, interactive features, scripts, forms, audio and video are not preserved. DRM is not removed. Protected books, fixed layouts, KFX, unrelated PRC files and unsupported compression variants receive an explanation. Browser resources determine practical limits; there is no fixed file-size or chapter-count quota.

To read the original file, use the [MOBI & AZW3 Reader](/tools/mobi-reader/). To inspect the downloaded book, use the [EPUB Reader](/tools/epub-reader/). For plain text or HTML instead, use the [Ebook to TXT / HTML Converter](/tools/ebook-text-html-export/).
