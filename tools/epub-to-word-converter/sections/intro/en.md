## How to convert EPUB to Word

Choose an `.epub` file or drop it onto the converter. Preview the chapters, then choose **Download DOCX**. For example, `field-guide.epub` becomes `field-guide.docx`. The conversion runs on your device.

## Edit ebook text in a DOCX document

Open the `.docx` in Microsoft Word or a compatible editor to edit or reuse the text. The converter keeps chapter order, headings, lists, tables, links, and supported illustrations. The document uses the title and author from the EPUB when available.

## Which EPUB books work best

Use an unencrypted reflowable EPUB, the kind that lets you adjust the font size. The Word document uses an editable layout, so pagination and line wrapping may change. Open the result in the [Microsoft Word Viewer](/tools/microsoft-word-viewer/) to inspect the converted document.
