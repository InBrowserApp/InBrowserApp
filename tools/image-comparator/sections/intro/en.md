## How to compare two images

Choose or drop the original image under **Before** and the revised image under **After**.

- **Slider** reveals one version on each side of the divider. Drag it across the same area to compare details, such as a photo before and after a color edit.
- **Overlay** blends the two images. Start at 50% opacity to see whether text or edges have shifted.
- **Pixel difference** highlights small changes that can be difficult to spot by eye, including changes in transparency.

## How to read the pixel difference map

Pink marks pixels that differ at the current tolerance. The count and percentage use the images’ original pixel dimensions. Keep the tolerance at 0% to catch subtle changes, or raise it to ignore small variations, such as color changes from JPEG compression.

## Comparing images of different sizes

Slider and Overlay align images at the top left and preserve their proportions. Pixel difference requires matching widths and heights. For screenshots, use the same window size and browser zoom, or use the [Image Cropper](../image-cropper/) to crop both versions to the same area.
