## Sort lines of text

Paste one item per line, choose **Ascending** for A to Z or **Descending** for Z to A, then copy the sorted text. Use it to organize names, filenames, tags, or one-column lists.

## Clean up the list while sorting

Turn on **Trim each line** to remove spaces at the edges and **Remove blank lines** to skip empty or whitespace-only rows. With **Ignore case** and **Remove duplicate lines** on, the list `pear`, `Apple`, `apple` becomes `Apple`, `pear` in ascending order. The first spelling of each duplicate is kept.

## How numbers and mixed case are sorted

The sorter compares text character by character using UTF-16 order. For example, `10` comes before `2`. Turn on **Ignore case** to sort uppercase and lowercase spellings together. A final line break counts as an empty line, which you can drop with **Remove blank lines**.
