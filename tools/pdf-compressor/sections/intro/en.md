## How to compress a scanned PDF

Reduce the size of scanned forms, receipts, and reports before emailing them or uploading them to a portal. PDF Compressor works on compatible images embedded in a PDF, which often take up most of the space in a document from a scanner.

1. Choose a PDF or drop it into the tool.
2. Start with **Balanced** and select **Compress PDF**.
3. Compare **Original size**, **Download size**, and **Size reduction**. If you have an attachment limit, check the download size against that limit.
4. Download the file and open it to check small print, stamps, or handwriting. You can switch levels and compress the original again.

## Choose a compression level

- **Higher quality** keeps more detail in fine print, diagrams, and scans you plan to print.
- **Balanced** is a starting point for everyday scans such as forms, invoices, and reports.
- **Smallest file** reduces image resolution and quality further. Check important details in the downloaded PDF if you use it to meet a tight upload limit.

## Will the PDF text stay searchable?

Yes, where the original already contains text. The tool replaces compatible embedded images while keeping the existing page content, including selectable text and an OCR text layer. A scan made entirely of images still needs OCR before its words can be selected or searched.

## Why did my PDF stay the same size?

PDFs made mostly of text or vector graphics, already optimized scans, and PDFs with unsupported image encodings may have little or no room to shrink. Some images, including those with transparency or specialized color profiles, are kept as they are. If the result would be as large as the original or larger, the download uses the original file. The tool also keeps the original for digitally signed PDFs, PDF/A archives, PDF 2.x, and files saved in multiple revisions. Very large individual images may be skipped to keep browser memory use manageable. Unlock password-protected PDFs before compressing them.

## Compress PDFs locally after editing

Your browser reads and compresses the PDF on your device. Your document stays off the server, and the downloaded copy is separate from the source. If you need to [merge PDFs](/tools/pdf-merger/), [split a PDF](/tools/pdf-splitter/), or [reorder and rotate PDF pages](/tools/pdf-page-organizer/), finish those edits first. Then open the exported PDF here and check its download size before sharing it.
