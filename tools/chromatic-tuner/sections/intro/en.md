## Tune an instrument with your microphone

Press **Start microphone**, allow access and play one steady note. The tuner shows the detected note, its frequency in hertz, and how far it is from the target in cents. If it says **Flat**, raise the pitch. If it says **Sharp**, lower it. The **In tune** reading means you are within 5 cents of the target. The microphone signal is analyzed in your browser.

## Choose a target note or string

Use **Chromatic** to tune to the nearest note. For a specific string, choose **Guitar** or **Ukulele**, then pick the string you are tuning. The guitar preset uses standard E–A–D–G–B–E tuning from low to high. The ukulele preset uses high G–C–E–A. Pluck one string at a time so ringing strings and background sound do not compete with the note you want to tune.

## Use a reference tone and calibrate A4

Select **Play reference tone** to hear your selected string, or A4 in chromatic mode. The default **A4 reference (Hz)** is 440 Hz. Set it to match another instrument, such as a piano tuned to A4 at 442 Hz. One semitone is 100 cents, so a reading of 10 cents sharp is one tenth of a semitone above your target.
