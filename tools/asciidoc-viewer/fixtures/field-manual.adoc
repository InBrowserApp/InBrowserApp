== Prepare the station

NOTE: Record the station identifier before starting a measurement.

WARNING: Do not enter the water during a flood.

* Check the gauge.
* Prepare equipment.
** Use a clean sampling bottle.
** Keep the notebook dry.

. Record the time.
. Collect the sample.
. Review the reading.

=== Record a measurement

.Sample command
[source,shell]
----
observe --station river-north --sample temperature --format json --notes "A wide command line stays inside its own scrolling area" # <1>
----
<1> The station identifier follows the printed gauge label.
