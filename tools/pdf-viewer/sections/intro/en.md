## Read a PDF in your browser

Open a local PDF or drop it into the viewer. Navigate between pages, adjust the zoom, or fit the page to the available width. Select text to copy it, or search the document and move between matches.

## Privacy and compatibility

Files and passwords are processed locally and are not uploaded or saved by this tool. The viewer supports files up to 50 MB and 1,000 pages. Password-protected PDFs can be opened with their password. Embedded fonts and bundled PDF resources are used where available; missing fonts may affect the appearance of some documents.

Image-only scans can be read, but text selection and search require an existing text layer. This viewer does not perform OCR, edit documents, or run embedded scripts. Document links open only when you choose them.
