## Read a Word document in your browser

Open a local .docx file or drop it into the viewer. Navigate pages, zoom or fit the page to the available width, select text to copy, and search the document. Tables, images, headers, and footers are displayed where supported.

## Privacy and compatibility

Documents are processed locally and are not uploaded or saved by this tool. Fonts are taken from the document or your device; no online fonts are requested. Font substitution and unsupported features can change line wrapping and pagination compared with desktop Word.

Files are limited to 50 MB and 1,000 pages. Archive expansion and rendered page sizes are also limited to keep browser memory bounded. Encrypted documents, legacy .doc files, templates, and macro-enabled files are not supported. The viewer does not edit, export, or print documents, run macros, or load external document resources.
