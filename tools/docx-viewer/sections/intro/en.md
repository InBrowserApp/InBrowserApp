## Read a Word document in your browser

Open a local .docx, .docm, .dotx, or .dotm file or drop it into the viewer. Documents and templates open as saved, read-only content. Navigate pages, zoom or fit the page to the available width, select text to copy, and search the document. Tables, images, headers, and footers are displayed where supported. Use Export Markdown to prepare a text copy, then Download Markdown to save it.

## Privacy and compatibility

Documents are processed locally and are not uploaded or saved by this tool. Fonts are taken from the document or your device; no online fonts are requested. Font substitution and unsupported features can change line wrapping and pagination compared with desktop Word.

Macro-enabled documents and templates can be viewed, but their macros never run. Content that depends on macros, form automation, or external data is not updated. Templates open without creating a new document.

Archive expansion and rendered page sizes are limited to keep browser memory bounded. Encrypted documents and legacy .doc files are not supported. The viewer does not edit or print documents or load external document resources.

Markdown export keeps text and basic structure, with headers, footers, notes, and comments in separate sections. It does not preserve page layout or fonts. Images, charts, equations, and unsupported shapes use omission markers; complex tables and tracked changes are simplified. Review important content against the original.

To download a supported document as an image-based PDF, use the [DOCX to PDF Converter](../docx-to-pdf-converter/) and check the output appearance before downloading.
