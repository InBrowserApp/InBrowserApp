## Export the content of a local ebook

Open an unencrypted EPUB, MOBI/AZW3 or FB2 book and choose HTML or TXT. The converter also recognizes supported AZW and PRC book containers, plus FBZ and FB2.ZIP archives containing one book. Everything is processed in your browser; no account, upload or remote conversion service is involved.

## Choose HTML or plain text

HTML is a portable reading copy with selectable text, chapter headings, internal links and supported included images. TXT keeps readable text in UTF-8, with chapter boundaries and safe external link addresses. Images and formatting are omitted from TXT. Available title and author information are included.

Main chapters keep their reading order. Available footnotes and supporting sections remain included. Navigation documents and repeated chapter entries are not exported as duplicate chapters. A missing required chapter stops the export; unavailable optional resources are identified instead of silently presented as complete.

## Compatibility and reading copies

This is content extraction, not a reproduction of the original pagination. Publisher styles, interactive features, scripts, forms, audio, video and remote resources are not reproduced. DRM is not removed. Protected, fixed-layout and unsupported variants receive an explanation. Browser resources determine practical limits; there is no fixed file-size or chapter-count quota.

For reading controls and chapter navigation, use the [EPUB Reader](/tools/epub-reader/), [MOBI & AZW3 Reader](/tools/mobi-reader/) or [FB2 Reader](/tools/fb2-reader/).
