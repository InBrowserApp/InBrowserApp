## Open an IGS or IGES file online

Choose **Open files** or drop an `.igs` or `.iges` CAD model onto the viewer. Both extensions identify the IGES interchange format. Choose **Try an example** to explore a stepped part. The viewer reads your file on your device.

## Inspect model surfaces in 3D

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag or drag with two fingers to pan. Choose **Fit to view** to frame the model, **Wireframe** to see the display triangles, or **Enter full screen** for more room. The viewer turns supported IGES surfaces into a triangle mesh for display and uses colors included by the importer. Export surfaces from your CAD application for an IGES file that contains only curves. [OpenCascade IGES importer](https://github.com/kovacsv/occt-import-js)

## Measure distances and switch units

**Overall size** gives the extents along the model’s X, Y and Z axes. **Measurement unit** switches between millimeters, meters and inches. Choose **Measure** and pick two points on the rendered surfaces to get a straight-line distance. Measurements on curved surfaces are approximate because they use the displayed triangles. Check critical dimensions in the source CAD model.

## Save a PNG or open a related CAD file

Set the view angle and zoom, then choose **Save PNG** to capture the current view for a drawing review or build note. Open STEP or STP versions of CAD parts and assemblies in the [STEP Viewer](../step-viewer/).
