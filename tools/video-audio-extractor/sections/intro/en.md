## Extract audio from MP4 or WebM

Save a soundtrack, narration, or alternate language track from a video as a
separate audio file. Open an MP4 or WebM, choose the track you want to keep,
and listen before downloading. Processing takes place in your browser, so
your video and extracted audio stay on your device.

### How to extract an audio track

1. Select **Choose MP4 or WebM** or drop a local video onto the tool.
2. Choose an **Audio track**. Each option shows its codec and any available
   language or track name. This helps you find commentary or a different
   language in a video with multiple audio tracks.
3. Play the **Audio preview** to confirm you picked the right track, then
   download the audio file. Repeat with another track if you need it separately.

### Which audio formats can I download?

| Source | Audio codec    | Download        |
| ------ | -------------- | --------------- |
| MP4    | AAC            | M4A             |
| WebM   | Opus or Vorbis | Audio-only WebM |

The extractor copies the encoded audio into a new container, a process called
remuxing. The downloaded file keeps the original audio quality and excludes
the video and other audio tracks.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I extract audio from MP4 to M4A without losing quality?

Yes. When an MP4 contains an AAC track, the tool saves the original AAC
audio in an M4A container without re-encoding.

#### Why is the preview unavailable?

Preview playback depends on your browser's support for the extracted audio
format. You can still download a supported track if the preview cannot play.
If the tool reports an unsupported codec, choose another track if available.
