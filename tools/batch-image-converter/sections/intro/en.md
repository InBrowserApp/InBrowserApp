## How to convert images in bulk

Drop or choose multiple images, even if they use different formats. Select JPG, PNG or WebP, then convert the batch. Download all successful conversions as a ZIP or save individual results. Files are processed locally in your browser.

## Supported input formats

Convert HEIC and HEIF photos, TIFF and BigTIFF pages, JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, WebP, AVIF stills, ICO variants, JPEG XL, and JPEG 2000 still images (JP2 and J2K). Supported pages, variants and frames become separate still images. Animated PNG exports its default still image. Timed HEIF/AVIF and JPEG 2000 compositions are unsupported.

## Quality and transparency

JPG fills transparent areas with white. PNG and WebP keep supported transparency. JPG and WebP offer adjustable quality, while PNG saves without lossy compression. Exports keep the oriented pixel dimensions and use 8-bit color. For a custom JPG background or to preview and export one specific page or frame, use the [Image to JPG Converter](/tools/image-to-jpg-converter/).
