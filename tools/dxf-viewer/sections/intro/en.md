## Open a DXF drawing online

Choose **Open DXF file** or drop an ASCII `.dxf` file onto the viewer. The drawing is read locally in your browser. Choose **Try an example** to open a bracket drawing with a profile, drill holes, centerlines and labels.

## Zoom the drawing and switch layers

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use **Fit drawing** to show the full drawing again. Open **Layers** to show or hide individual layers from the file. For example, hide the centerlines while checking hole outlines, then turn the centerlines back on to read their alignment.

## See which DXF files open

The viewer draws common 2D model-space content such as lines, polylines, arcs, circles, blocks and supported text. Export as ASCII DXF if your CAD program offers a binary DXF option. For a drawing stored only on a layout sheet, copy the relevant geometry into model space before exporting. Text appearance depends on the available fonts. Convert labels to outlines in your CAD program if their exact appearance matters.

DXF stores drawing information as entities and related tables. See the [Autodesk DXF reference](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2017/ENU/AutoCAD-DXF/files/index.htm) for details.

## Inspect the original CAD model

Use the [STEP Viewer](../step-viewer/) to inspect a solid part or assembly in 3D when you have the original STEP or STP file.
