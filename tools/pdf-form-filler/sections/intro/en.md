## How to fill a PDF form

Open or drop a fillable PDF, then select the highlighted fields where they appear on the page. You can type into text boxes, tick checkboxes, pick a radio button, or choose an item from a drop-down or list. Press Tab to move between fields on the current page. For longer forms, use the page controls to move through the document. You can try the sample before opening your own form.

The PDF and your answers stay in your browser. Both downloads create a new PDF and leave the file you opened unchanged.

## Editable PDF or flattened PDF?

- **Download editable PDF** saves your answers and keeps the existing form fields. Choose it when you want to finish later or pass the form to someone else to fill in.
- **Download flattened PDF** makes the visible field contents part of the page and removes the interactive fields. Choose it when you need a finished copy to send, print, or archive. For example, you can keep an editable copy of a completed registration form and send a flattened copy to the organizer.

Flattening applies to the form fields. It does not password-protect the PDF or make it tamper-proof.

## Why can't I type into the PDF?

This tool fills standard interactive PDF forms, also called AcroForms. A scanned form or a PDF with printed boxes may look fillable even when it has no interactive fields. Ask the sender for a fillable PDF or use a PDF annotation tool to place text on the page. For an XFA form, use a viewer that supports XFA or ask for an AcroForm version.
