## Convert a paper to PDF

Select or drop a CAJ, KDH, or NH document. Conversion runs in your browser. Check the page preview, then choose Download PDF to save a copy with the original filename and a .pdf extension. Available text and bookmarks are retained. Replacing or closing the document cancels unfinished work.

## Check compatibility

CAJ files with PDF page objects and KDH files with PDF content are supported. Image-based HN-A/NH and C8 variants are experimental: compare important equations, tables, and diagrams with the original. Native HN-B/C8 text requiring separate fonts, protected files, TEB variants, damaged documents, and CAA reference files are not supported. Incomplete conversions are not offered for download.

Scanned pages remain images; no OCR is performed. Some characters may use substitute glyphs, and some bookmarks may be adjusted or unavailable. Review the conversion notes before sharing the PDF.

## Keep your document local

Your paper is not uploaded and no account is needed. There is no fixed file-size or page-count quota, although large documents can exceed browser resources. Closing the document releases its preview and download. The CAJ Viewer also provides PDF download when you are already reading a paper.
