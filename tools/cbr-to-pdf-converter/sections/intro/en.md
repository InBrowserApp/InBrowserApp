## How to convert CBR to PDF

Choose a `.cbr` file or drag it onto the converter. It turns each comic image into one PDF page. Check the result in the preview, then choose **Download PDF**. For example, `volume-01.cbr` becomes `volume-01.pdf`, ready to read on a tablet, share, or print. A `.rar` archive containing comic images works too.

## What happens to the comic pages?

A CBR file is a RAR archive of comic pages. The converter reads folders and filenames in natural order, so `chapter2/page2.jpg` comes before `chapter2/page10.jpg`. It keeps portrait pages and widescreen spreads in their original proportions, without cropping or resampling. Supported images include JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP, and AVIF where your browser can decode it.

The artwork and lettering remain images. To search the dialogue, download the PDF and open it in [PDF OCR](/tools/pdf-ocr/). Choose the language used in the comic so OCR can add a searchable text layer.

## If a comic does not open

The conversion runs on your device. Large solid RAR archives may take longer to read. For a password-protected or split archive, unpack it first and use a single readable archive. If you only want to browse the original comic, use the [CBR Comic Reader](/tools/cbr-reader/). For a ZIP-based comic, use [CBZ to PDF](/tools/cbz-to-pdf-converter/).
