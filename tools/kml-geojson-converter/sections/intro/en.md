## How to convert KML and GeoJSON

Choose KML → GeoJSON or GeoJSON → KML. Paste the source text or open a file, then convert it. Copy the result or download it as a .geojson or .kml file for QGIS, Google Earth or a web map.

## What the conversion preserves

Both formats store WGS 84 coordinates in longitude, latitude order. The converter carries over points, lines, polygons and placemark metadata such as names and descriptions. KML folders, styles, overlays and network links may be omitted. The tool opens plain KML files and processes them locally in your browser.
