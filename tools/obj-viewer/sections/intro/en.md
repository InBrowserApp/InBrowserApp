## Open a Wavefront OBJ file online

Drop an `.obj` file onto the viewer or choose it from your device. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag or drag with two fingers to pan. Choose **Fit to view** to frame the complete model, or switch to **Wireframe** to inspect its triangles. Choose **Try an example** to see a small mounting bracket.

## Preview OBJ materials and textures

A Wavefront OBJ file stores the geometry and can reference one or more `.mtl` material libraries. An MTL can in turn reference image files. Select or drop the OBJ, its MTL files and the texture images together. For example, open `chair.obj`, `chair.mtl` and `wood.jpg` in one selection when those are the names used by the model. Files stay on your device.

An OBJ still opens when its companion files are missing. The affected surfaces appear with a default material or without the missing texture. If a texture is missing, check that its filename matches the reference in the MTL, including the extension, then open the model and companion files together again.

## View related 3D formats

For a triangular mesh exported for 3D printing, use the [STL Viewer](../stl-viewer/). If you have the original CAD part or assembly as a `.step` or `.stp` file, open it in the [STEP Viewer](../step-viewer/).
