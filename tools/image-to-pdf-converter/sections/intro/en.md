Combine local photos, scans and selected pages into one PDF without uploading files. Each selected image becomes a PDF page, in the order shown in the queue.

## Formats and source pages

Open JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, WebP, AVIF still images, TIFF, ICO, HEIC/HEIF still images and collections, JPEG XL, JP2 and JPEG 2000 codestreams (J2K). The file contents are checked; changing an extension does not make a file compatible. Availability depends on the encoding inside the file.

Multi-page TIFFs appear as individual pages. Collections, icon variants and composited GIF/WebP/JXL animation frames also appear separately. All readable and unreadable entries are listed, and all entries start selected. Deselect or remove anything you do not want in the PDF, including unwanted frames or icon sizes. An unreadable selected entry must be removed or deselected before export. Animated PNG offers only its default still image, labeled as a poster; PDFs do not preserve animation.

## Selection, order and layout

Mix supported formats, select pages with their checkboxes, change the queue order and rotate individual images before generating. The saved PDF follows the displayed order of selected entries. Thumbnails are reduced for browsing; export uses the full decoded pixel dimensions with source orientation and your rotation applied.

Choose page size, margins, orientation, fit and JPEG quality. Auto orientation follows the rotated image dimensions. Contain keeps the whole image inside the margin; cover fills the printable area and may crop the edges. Transparent areas become white. Pictures are re-encoded as 8-bit sRGB JPEGs: compression is lossy, high bit depth and HDR are reduced, and colors may differ from a color-managed editor. This is an image PDF; it does not add OCR or searchable text.

## Privacy, cancellation and limits

Files and processing stay on this device. Cancel stops the current operation, while clear removes the queue and any previous download. Changing the queue or output settings invalidates the previous PDF. Unsupported inputs and unreadable pages are identified in the queue; an export error never offers an incomplete PDF as a successful result.

HEIF/AVIF timed sequences, JPEG 2000 compositions (JPX/JPF/JPM), Motion JPEG 2000 (MJ2), SVG and document formats are not supported here. Use the dedicated converters for other formats. Very large or complex images may exceed browser or decoder resources; there is no fixed file-size or page-count cap. Decoder licenses and corresponding source information are linked in the tool.
