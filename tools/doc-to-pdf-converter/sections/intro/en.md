## Convert legacy documents to PDF

Open a local Word 97–2003 DOC or compatible WPS / WPT document. Supported body text, tables, and embedded raster images are reflowed onto A4 portrait pages with 18 mm margins. Explicit page breaks are retained where recovered. Inspect the generated PDF with page navigation, zoom, or focus mode, then download it.

## Appearance and compatibility

The PDF contains image pages at 150 DPI with a white background. Text is not selectable or searchable, and OCR is not added. This is a reflowed copy: original page sizes, columns, positioning, fonts, spacing, and page count are not reproduced exactly. Saved field text is used without recalculation. The final revision view keeps inserted text and removes deleted text.

Detected automatic numbering, headers, footers, footnotes, endnotes, comments, text boxes, embedded objects, missing images, and unsupported content stop conversion without a partial download. Manually typed list numbers remain ordinary text. Complex documents should be exported from their original Office application. Older proprietary WPS, Microsoft Works, HTML-in-OLE, and password-protected documents are not supported. Templates export their saved content. Macros are never executed; interactive links and digital signatures are not preserved or verified.

## Local processing

Documents stay in this browser. Remote document resources and fonts are not fetched. Canceling, replacing, or closing a document discards its result and releases processing resources. There is no fixed file-size or page-count quota; actual browser memory and image budgets still apply.

For reading the original file, use the [DOC / WPS Viewer](../doc-viewer/). For modern Word files, use the [DOCX to PDF Converter](../docx-to-pdf-converter/).
