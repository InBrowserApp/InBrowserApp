## Open and read an MBOX file

An MBOX file stores multiple email messages in a single mailbox archive. Drop the file above or choose it from your device, then select a message from the list. Search by subject, sender, or recipient to find a conversation. You can read HTML and plain-text messages, check who sent them and when, and download attachments from the selected message.

## Find the mailbox in Gmail, Thunderbird, or Apple Mail exports

For Gmail, export Mail with Google Takeout and extract the downloaded archive. Look for an .mbox file inside Takeout/Mail. In Thunderbird, a mailbox file can have no extension. In an Apple Mail export, open the .mbox folder and select the file named mbox. Open the mailbox file itself after extracting any ZIP or TGZ download.

## Read your email privately in the browser

Your browser indexes the mailbox on your device and reads individual messages as you select them. Email and attachments stay on your device. The HTML preview blocks external content such as tracking images. Very large mailboxes can take longer to index, depending on your device and browser.
