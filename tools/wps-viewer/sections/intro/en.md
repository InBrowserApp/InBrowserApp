## Read a WPS Writer file in your browser

Open or drop a compatible WPS Writer document (`.wps`) or template (`.wpt`). Read selectable text in a continuous layout, zoom, or navigate through recognized headings. Your file stays on your device.

## Compatibility and related tools

Supported files contain Word binary or HTML document content in an OLE container. Older proprietary WPS formats and Microsoft Works files are unsupported. Complex formatting may be simplified.

To export a compatible file, use the [WPS to PDF Converter](../wps-to-pdf-converter/). For `.doc` and `.docx` files, use the [Microsoft Word Viewer](../microsoft-word-viewer/).
