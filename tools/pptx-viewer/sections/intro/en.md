## Read a presentation in your browser

Open a local .pptx file or drop it into the viewer. Navigate slides, adjust zoom, select text to copy, and search the presentation. Text, shapes, tables, and images are displayed where supported.

## Privacy and compatibility

Presentations are processed locally and are not uploaded or saved by this tool. Fonts come from the presentation or your device; no online fonts are requested. Font substitution and unsupported features can affect layout. Animations, transitions, embedded media playback, and presenter mode are not included.

Files are limited to 50 MB and 1,000 slides. Archive expansion and rendered slide sizes are also limited. Encrypted presentations, legacy .ppt files, templates, and macro-enabled files are not supported. The viewer does not edit, export, or print presentations, run macros, or load external document resources.
