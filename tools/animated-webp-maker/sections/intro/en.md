## Make an animation from images

Add at least two PNG, JPEG, WebP, or AVIF images. Drag the frames or use the
arrows to change their order. Set the duration for each frame, or use **Apply
to all** for a consistent pace. Set the loop count to 0 to repeat forever or
1 to play once.

## Preview and download

Create the animated WebP animation, select **Play** to preview it, and download
the result. Frames are centered on a shared canvas with transparent padding.
The export preserves partial transparency. Your browser processes everything
on your device.
