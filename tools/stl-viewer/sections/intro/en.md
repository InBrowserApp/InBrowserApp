## Open an STL file online

Drop an `.stl` file onto the viewer or choose it from your device. Both ASCII and binary STL files open in the browser, and your model stays on your device. Choose **Try an example** to explore a small mounting bracket first.

## Inspect the 3D model before printing

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag or drag with two fingers to pan. Use **Fit to view** to bring the whole model back into frame. Turn on **Wireframe** to see the triangles that make up the surface. This can reveal how finely a curved surface was exported.

The size values show the complete span along X, Y and Z. STL stores coordinates without a unit, so a span of 40 can mean 40 mm or 40 inches depending on the export settings. Confirm the unit when you import the model into your slicer.

## View other 3D file formats

STL describes the surface of a part with triangles. For a Wavefront file with a companion material library, use the [OBJ Viewer](../obj-viewer/). For a CAD exchange file containing an assembly or solid model, use the [STEP Viewer](../step-viewer/).
