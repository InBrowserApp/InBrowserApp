## Add a watermark to selected PDF pages

Upload a PDF, enter text or choose a PNG or JPEG image, and adjust the watermark in the live preview. Choose a single watermark or a tiled pattern, set its rotation and opacity, and enter page ranges such as `1-3,5`. Leave the range blank to watermark every page.

## Private processing, original content preserved

Your PDF and watermark are processed locally in your browser. Download a new copy with the watermark added over the selected pages; other pages keep their original content. Transparent PNGs work well for logos. Text is rendered as a high-resolution transparent image using your browser's fonts, including supported non-Latin scripts. The original PDF pages are not converted to images. Watermarks identify a document but do not prevent copying or editing. Encrypted PDFs must be unlocked first, and editing a digitally signed PDF invalidates its signature.
