## How to convert FB2 to PDF

Open a `.fb2` book or drop it onto the converter. An `.fb2.zip` or `.fbz` archive with one book also works. Check the chapter preview and pick **A4**, **A5**, or **US Letter**. Select **Save as PDF**, choose the PDF destination in your browser’s print dialog, and save. To remove the date and address, turn off browser headers and footers.

## What happens to the book’s text?

The PDF keeps the original text selectable and searchable, including Cyrillic. It uses a simple print layout with the chapter order, verse lines, available cover and embedded illustrations, and links to readable notes. Page breaks follow your chosen paper size. Words that were drawn inside an image stay part of the image.

## When to use a different format

Choose PDF to print the book or share a fixed-page copy. For adjustable text on an e-reader, try [FB2 to EPUB](../fb2-to-epub-converter/). If you only want to open the book, use the [FB2 Reader](../fb2-reader/). Your book stays on your device.
