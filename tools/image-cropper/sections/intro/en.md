## How to crop an image

Choose or drop a local image, then drag the selection or its handles. Use Free to choose any aspect ratio, Original to keep the image's proportions, or a preset such as 1:1, 4:3, or 16:9. Pixel fields let you enter the selection's position and size precisely. Rotate in 90° steps, flip horizontally or vertically, and select Crop image to preview and download the result.

## Quality and privacy

Everything runs in your browser; image contents are never uploaded. The preview fits your screen, while exports use the original image pixels without upscaling. PNG preserves transparency; JPEG uses a white background; WebP supports transparency and adjustable quality. If a browser cannot encode the selected format, the download uses the format actually produced.

## Format notes

PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, and BMP input depend on your browser's decoding support. Animated images produce a single still frame. Exports do not preserve the original metadata, animation, or all color-profile information. Very large images can exceed available browser memory. Use Image Resizer when you need to scale the whole image instead of selecting an area.
