## Generate a batch of sample phone numbers

Choose a country or region, pick a number format, enter the quantity, and select **Generate**. Copy the results into a form or download a UTF-8 text file with one number per line. Use the numbers to check form input masks, populate sample contacts, or exercise how your app stores and displays phone numbers.

## Choose national or international format

**National** uses a format from the selected country's Faker.js locale. For example, a US number can appear as `(202) 555-0100`, while a UK number can appear as `07700 900123`. **International (E.164)** writes a plus sign, the country calling code, and the remaining digits without spaces, such as `+12025550100` or `+447700900123`.

## Keep the plus sign and leading zeros

Store phone numbers as text in spreadsheets and databases. A spreadsheet that imports `07700 900123` or `+447700900123` as a number can lose the leading zero or plus sign. These are sample numbers for format testing. To verify calls or SMS, use a test number you control.
