## How to convert Chinese characters to Pinyin

Type or paste simplified or traditional Chinese into the Chinese text box. The tool shows the Mandarin pronunciation in Hanyu Pinyin as you type. Choose a tone format, then use **Copy pinyin** to take the result into a document, a flashcard, or a message. The conversion runs in your browser and your text stays on your device.

## Which tone format should I use?

- **Tone marks** give you `nǐ hǎo` for `你好`. The marks over the vowels are useful when reading aloud or studying Mandarin pronunciation.
- **Tone numbers** give you `ni3 hao3`. The numbers `1` through `4` stand for the four tones. A `0` marks a neutral tone, as in `ma1 ma0` for `妈妈`.
- **No tones** gives you `ni hao`. Use this format when you only need the syllables. Letters such as `ü` stay in the result.

## How does it handle polyphonic characters and mixed text?

The converter uses common phrases to choose a reading. For example, `行` is `háng` in `银行` and `xíng` in `行走`. A single character can have several valid readings. Paste the surrounding phrase for more context and double-check names or uncommon words.

The output separates syllables with spaces and keeps punctuation, line breaks, and non-Chinese text. For instance, `你好，Lisa！` becomes `nǐ hǎo，Lisa！` with tone marks.
