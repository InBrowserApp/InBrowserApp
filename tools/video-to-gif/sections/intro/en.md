## Turn a video clip into a GIF

Choose a local video, then drag the trim handles or enter the start and end in seconds. Select **Make GIF**, play the preview, and download the result. The GIF loops without sound, and the video stays on your device.

For a small GIF to share in a chat, try a three-second clip at 10 FPS and 480 px wide. Use a higher frame rate for smoother motion, or reduce the width or frame rate to make the file smaller. If the video cannot be opened, try an MP4 or WebM that plays in your browser.
