## Convert MP4 or WebM to an animated GIF

Turn a short video clip into a looping GIF for a chat, a bug report, or a quick
tutorial. Choose just the part you want to share, then set its frame rate and
width. The source video and the exported GIF are processed in your browser.

### How to turn a video into a GIF

1. Select **Choose video** or drop a video into the upload area. Use the
   source player to find the part you want to keep.
2. Drag the two trim handles or enter the start and end in seconds. For example,
   enter **0.5** and **3.5** to keep three seconds starting half a second into
   the video.
3. Set the frame rate and width. At **12 FPS**, that three-second clip gives you
   36 frames. The height adjusts automatically to preserve the video's
   proportions.
4. Select **Make GIF**, then play the preview. Check the dimensions and file
   size shown below the preview heading before selecting **Download GIF**.

### How to make a smaller GIF

Start with a short clip at **10 FPS** and a width of **480 px** or less. Cut any
pause before or after the action first. Reduce the width if small details are
still readable in the preview, or lower the frame rate if smoother motion
matters less. A GIF can be larger than its source MP4 because MP4 compresses
motion more efficiently.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I convert MOV as well as MP4 and WebM?

The browser must be able to decode the video. Two files with the same extension
can use different codecs, so an MP4 or MOV may play on one device and fail on
another. If the file won't open, try a browser-playable MP4 or WebM.

#### Does the GIF have sound?

The exported GIF loops silently. It contains the selected video frames, and the
source file stays on your device.
