## How to convert EPUB to KEPUB

Choose or drop a reflowable `.epub` book. When conversion finishes, select **Download KEPUB**. For example, `night-garden.epub` becomes `night-garden.kepub.epub`. The book stays on your device throughout the conversion.

## Add the converted book to your Kobo

Connect your Kobo eReader over USB and copy the `.kepub.epub` file to its drive. Keep both parts of the file ending so the reader recognizes it as a Kobo EPUB. Eject the drive safely and let the Kobo import the book.

## What changes when you convert an EPUB

The converter adds Kobo reading-position markers and keeps the book’s text, table of contents, cover, and bundled styles and images. Use an unencrypted, reflowable EPUB, the kind that lets you change the font size. You can also open the downloaded book in the [EPUB Reader](/tools/epub-reader/) to check its text and navigation.
