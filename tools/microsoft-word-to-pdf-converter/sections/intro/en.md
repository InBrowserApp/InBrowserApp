## Convert a Word document to PDF

Open or drop a `.doc`, `.docx`, `.docm`, `.dotx`, or `.dotm` file. Preview the generated PDF before downloading. The file is processed in your browser.

## Page layout and compatibility

DOC body content is reflowed onto A4 pages. The newer formats use the document’s page size and orientation. PDF pages are images at 150 DPI. Encryption and some document features can prevent conversion.

For reading, use the [Microsoft Word Viewer](../microsoft-word-viewer/). To convert a compatible `.wps` or `.wpt` file, use the [WPS to PDF Converter](../wps-to-pdf-converter/).
