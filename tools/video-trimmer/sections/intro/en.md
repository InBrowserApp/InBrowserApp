## How to trim an MP4 or WebM video

1. Choose or drop a video file. From [Screen Recorder](/tools/screen-recorder/), you can also select **Trim recording** when your recording is ready.
2. Drag the start and end handles or enter times in seconds. For example, enter **2.5** and **7.5** to keep a five-second clip. Select **Preview clip** to play that range.
3. Select an output format, then **Export clip** and **Download clip**.

## Which format should I choose?

Keep the original format or choose MP4 for broad playback compatibility. WebM is also useful for sharing on the web. Available exports depend on the video and audio codecs your browser supports. The video is processed on your device, and the trimmed clip is saved as a new file.
