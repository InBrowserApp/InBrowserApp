## How to convert MOBI to PDF

Open or drop a `.mobi` ebook. The converter also accepts compatible `.azw` and `.prc` books. Check the preview and pick **A4**, **A5**, or **US Letter**. Select **Save as PDF** and choose the PDF destination in your browser’s print dialog. For plain pages, turn off browser headers and footers.

## What stays in the PDF?

The book’s text stays selectable and searchable. The printable layout keeps available chapter headings, title, author, cover, and supported embedded illustrations. Lines and page breaks follow the paper size you choose. Text drawn into an illustration remains part of the image.

## Which Kindle and Mobipocket books work?

Use an unprotected MOBI book or an AZW or PRC file that contains a supported Kindle or Mobipocket ebook. Your book stays on your device. Read the source in the [MOBI Reader](../mobi-reader/) or use [MOBI to EPUB](../mobi-to-epub-converter/) for a reading copy with adjustable text.
