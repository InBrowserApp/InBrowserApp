## Trim and Merge Audio Locally

Keep the part you need from a voice note, song, or recording. Add one file to trim it, or add several files to join their selected regions in order. Your audio stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

## Select, Arrange, and Preview

Use the waveform handles or enter start and end times in seconds. Play each selection, move clips earlier or later, and create a WAV to preview the complete result before downloading. Reset a trim to restore the full clip, or undo an accidental removal.

## Formats and Audio Quality

Input support depends on your browser and the file’s codec. Common formats include MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC, and WebM. Mono and stereo audio is decoded at 48 kHz and exported as 16-bit PCM WAV. WAV avoids another lossy compression step, but does not restore detail lost in the source and can be larger than compressed files. Mixed mono and stereo clips export as stereo.

## Tips for Clean Cuts

Listen around the start and end of each selection. Enable smooth clip edges to apply a short 10 ms fade at each cut and reduce clicks. Clips join directly without overlap. Large recordings use more browser memory; work with shorter clips if your device struggles. Keep this page open until you have downloaded your result.
