## How to convert PDF to EPUB

Choose or drop a `.pdf` file, select **Convert to EPUB**, and then choose **Download EPUB**. For example, `field-notes.pdf` becomes `field-notes.epub`. Open the result in the [EPUB Reader](/tools/epub-reader/) or an eReader app to adjust the text size and check its reading order.

## Keep PDF text and recognize scanned pages

The converter keeps selectable text from the PDF. For pages that contain only a scan, choose the scan language before converting. Turn on **Also recognize English** for scans that mix another language with English. The browser downloads the OCR engine and language data as needed. Your PDF and recognized text stay on your device. A page stays as an image if OCR finds no text.

## Which PDFs work best as ebooks

Single-column books and documents with clear text work best. EPUB reflows text to fit your screen, so page breaks, complex tables, and multi-column layouts can change. To keep the original PDF layout while adding searchable text to scans, use [PDF OCR](/tools/pdf-ocr/).
