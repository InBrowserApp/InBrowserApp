## How to make a CBZ from images

Select **Choose images** for individual files or **Choose folder** for a folder of pages. You can also drop image files onto the converter. JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and AVIF pages can be packed into the same comic. Select **Create CBZ**, then **Download CBZ** to save `comic.cbz`. Your files stay on your device.

## Set the page order and cover

New pages are sorted by folder and filename, so `page2.jpg` comes before `page10.jpg`. Drag pages to rearrange them or use the up and down buttons. Select **Make cover** to move an image to the first page. Images added later go at the end of your current order.

## Read the finished comic

A CBZ is a ZIP archive of page images. The converter keeps the original image files and gives them numbered names so your comic reader follows the order you chose. Open the result in the [CBZ Comic Reader](/tools/cbz-reader/) or import it into your comic library.
