## Add or edit bookmarks in a PDF

Open a PDF to see its existing bookmarks, or start a new outline for a document that has none. Use the page preview to find a chapter or section, then select **Add bookmark**. The new bookmark points to the page you’re previewing. Give it a descriptive title so readers can find the section easily.

1. Select a bookmark to rename it or change its destination page. To link it to the page in the preview, select **Use preview page**.
2. Move bookmarks up or down to reorder them. Nest a bookmark under the previous one to group related sections, or move it up a level. Deleting a bookmark keeps its sub-bookmarks and moves them up a level.
3. Select **Download PDF**, then open the downloaded copy and look for the bookmarks or outline panel in your PDF reader.

## What are PDF bookmarks?

PDF bookmarks form a clickable outline in a PDF reader. Each bookmark can take readers straight to a chapter, appendix, or other destination. Nested bookmarks make it easier to navigate long PDFs such as manuals, reports, and course notes. Bookmarks live in the navigation panel, while a table of contents printed in the PDF is part of the page content.

## What changes in the downloaded PDF?

The new copy contains your edited bookmark tree. The page content and page order stay the same. Existing links and exact jump positions are retained until you change their destination. The tool reads and saves your PDF locally in your browser, without uploading the file.
