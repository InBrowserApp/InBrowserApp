## How to test your webcam online

Choose **Start camera** and allow camera access. A live preview appears with its resolution in pixels and its frame rate in frames per second (FPS). If the browser exposes more than one camera, use **Camera** to switch between them. Choose **Stop camera** to end the test.

## Requested resolution versus actual resolution

Use **Requested resolution** to try 720p, 1080p, or 4K. These are requested sizes. Compare them with **Actual resolution**, the dimensions of the video that reaches the browser. For example, a 1080p request can still produce 1280 × 720 if the camera, driver, connection, or browser selects a different mode. Some cameras offer a higher frame rate at a smaller size.

## How to interpret the webcam frame rate

**Preview frame rate** is measured from the frames presented by the browser. If the browser does not expose video frame timing, **Reported frame rate** shows the track's current configured FPS when available. Both describe the active session. Low light, a busy browser, or another app using the camera can affect the preview, so compare readings with the tab visible and under the lighting you plan to use.

## Camera permissions and privacy

The test uses your camera only after you start it. Video stays in the browser for the live preview. The page does not record it or ask for microphone access. If the preview fails to start, check the camera permission and system privacy settings, then close other apps using the camera and try again.
