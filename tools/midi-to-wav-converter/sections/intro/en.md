## Convert MIDI to WAV in your browser

Choose a .mid or .midi file, then select **Convert to WAV**. Listen to the result and download a 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo WAV file. WAV is useful when you want to share a backing track, import the audio into a video editor, or send someone a version they can play without installing MIDI instruments.

## How the instrument sounds are chosen

A MIDI file stores instructions such as notes, tempo, and instrument changes. A SoundFont supplies the instrument samples that turn those instructions into audio. The built-in GeneralUser GS SoundFont covers the standard General MIDI instruments and drums. To try a different piano, drum kit, or instrument bank, choose your own .sf2 or .sf3 file before converting. The WAV will capture the sounds of the selected SoundFont.

## Preview and download your WAV

The audio preview lets you check the mix before downloading. Rendering adds a short tail so notes and effects can decay after the last event. This tool renders MIDI files under 10 minutes and processes your MIDI and SoundFont locally. The built-in sound bank is fetched when you first use it.
