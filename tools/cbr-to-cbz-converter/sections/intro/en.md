## How to convert CBR to CBZ

Choose a `.cbr` comic or drop it onto the converter. A `.rar` archive containing comic page images works too. Check the first and last filenames, then choose **Download CBZ**. For example, `volume-01.cbr` becomes `volume-01.cbz`. The conversion runs on your device.

## Keep the original pages and comic metadata

CBR stores comic images in a RAR archive. CBZ uses ZIP, a format many comic readers and libraries can open. The converter copies the page files into the ZIP without re-encoding them, so their resolution and quality stay the same. It keeps folders and filenames in natural reading order, with `chapter2/page2.jpg` before `chapter2/page10.jpg`. Existing `ComicInfo.xml` files carry over as well.

## Open the new CBZ file

After downloading, open the result in the [CBZ Comic Reader](/tools/cbz-reader/) or import it into your comic library. If you need a printable version, use [CBZ to PDF](/tools/cbz-to-pdf-converter/). For a protected or split RAR archive, first unpack it and use a single unprotected copy.
