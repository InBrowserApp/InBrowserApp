## How to convert PowerPoint slides to images

Open or drop a PowerPoint file and review the slide preview. Choose PNG, JPG, or WebP, then select the current slide, an inclusive slide range, or the entire presentation. A single slide downloads as an image. Multiple slides are numbered in presentation order and downloaded together as a ZIP.

For example, choose slides 2 through 5 of `sales-deck.pptx` to save four images for a report. Use **2×** for more pixels when you need to crop the image or display it at a larger size. The slide proportions stay the same.

## When to choose PNG, JPG, or WebP

- **PNG** keeps text, diagrams, and flat colors crisp with lossless compression.
- **JPG** is useful for slides with photos when a smaller, widely compatible file matters.
- **WebP** is useful for publishing slide images on the web.

## What the PowerPoint export includes

The converter uses the saved slide layout, including hidden slides in the file order. Each image shows a static slide with a white background where the slide is transparent. Animations, transitions, speaker notes, and interactive content are omitted. Fonts and complex effects can differ from the original, so check the preview before downloading.

PPTX, PPTM, POTX, POTM, PPSX, and PPSM files are supported. The file stays in your browser, and macros are not run. Use the [PPTX Viewer](../pptx-viewer/) to read the full deck, or the [PPTX to PDF Converter](../pptx-to-pdf-converter/) to keep slides together in a printable document.
