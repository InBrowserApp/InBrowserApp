## How to convert EPUB to PDF

1. Select **Choose EPUB file** or drop a local `.epub` file onto the tool. Skim the preview to check the book's chapters and illustrations.
2. Choose a paper size. **A4** works for general printing, **A5** creates smaller pages for reading, and **US Letter** fits standard North American letter paper.
3. Select **Save as PDF**. In your browser's print dialog, choose **Save as PDF** as the destination and confirm. If the preview shows a date, address or page header, turn off **Headers and footers** in the print options.

For example, open `reading-notes.epub`, choose A5 and save it as `reading-notes.pdf`. You can then open the PDF in a standard viewer, share it or print it at that paper size.

## What the PDF keeps from the ebook

The PDF uses a simple, printable layout with the book's reading order, chapter headings, available cover and supported embedded images. Text from the EPUB stays selectable and searchable. A scan or illustration that already contains text remains an image.

EPUB text can reflow to fit different reading screens. PDF uses fixed pages, so line breaks, fonts and pagination can change during conversion. The browser's print preview shows the final page breaks. Publisher styles, embedded fonts and multimedia are omitted.

## Which EPUBs can I convert?

Use a DRM-free, reflowable EPUB. If the book has DRM or uses a fixed layout, such as some comics and illustrated textbooks, choose an unprotected reflowable edition. The converter runs in your browser and the ebook stays on your device. Remote images and other online book resources are not fetched.

Use the [EPUB Reader](../epub-reader/) to read and navigate the original ebook. To extract editable text or make a standalone HTML reading copy, use the [Ebook to Text / HTML Converter](../ebook-text-html-export/). Use the [PDF Viewer](../pdf-viewer/) to check the PDF after saving it.
