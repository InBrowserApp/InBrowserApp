## How to convert CBZ or CBR to EPUB

Choose a `.cbz` or `.cbr` comic or drop it onto the converter. ZIP or RAR archives containing comic page images also work. Check the cover and page count, then choose **Download EPUB**. For example, `volume-01.cbz` becomes `volume-01.epub`. The conversion runs on your device.

## Keep each comic page in its original layout

The converter uses fixed-layout EPUB 3 so each image takes one page. Page files follow natural order by folder and filename, and the first image becomes the cover. Portrait pages and wide spreads keep their proportions. Compatible image files can be kept as-is. Other pages your browser can read are saved as PNG.

## Read the EPUB comic

Open your file in an EPUB reader that supports fixed-layout books. The EPUB includes a table of contents with a link to each page. Manga lettering and other text in the artwork stay part of the page image.
