## How to extract frames from a video

Choose a video and select **Timestamps** to capture specific moments. For example, enter `0, 2.5, 01:23` to capture the first frame, the frame at two and a half seconds, and the frame at one minute and 23 seconds. You can also find a moment in the video preview and add its current time.

Choose **Fixed interval** to capture a sequence. For example, start at 10 seconds, end at 20 seconds and use a 5-second interval to get frames at 10, 15 and 20 seconds. The tool only samples times before the video ends.

## Choose an image format

Use JPG for smaller downloads or PNG for lossless images. The images keep the video’s displayed pixel dimensions. Download any frame from its preview, or choose **Download ZIP** to save the batch. Image filenames include the requested timestamp.

The tool uses your browser’s video decoder, so format support depends on the file’s codec and your browser. A timestamp selects the frame displayed at that time. The video and images are processed on your device.
