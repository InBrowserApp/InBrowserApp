## Turn a screenshot or photo into editable text

Use optical character recognition (OCR) to copy an error message from a screenshot, recover a quote from a photo, or extract text from a scanned page saved as an image.

1. Paste a screenshot, drop a file, or choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP image.
2. Search for the language in the image by name or Tesseract code, then select **Recognize text**. For mixed text, turn on **Also recognize English** to recognize English alongside the selected language.
3. Check the result against the preview. Edit any mistakes, then copy the text or download a UTF-8 TXT file.

## Get clearer OCR results

Use a sharp, upright image with good contrast and select a vertical model for vertically written Chinese, Japanese, or Korean. If the image contains a lot of unrelated detail, crop to the text you need. Give names, numbers, and punctuation an extra check before relying on the result. The output is plain text, so tables and page layouts may need manual cleanup.

## Where is my image processed?

Recognition runs in your browser. The OCR engine and the models for your selected languages load on demand. Your image and the extracted text stay on your device.
