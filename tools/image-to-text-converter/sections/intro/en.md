## Turn a screenshot or photo into editable text

Use optical character recognition (OCR) to copy an error message from a screenshot, recover a quote from a photo, or extract text from a scanned page saved as an image.

1. Paste a screenshot, drop a file, or choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP image.
2. Choose the language used in the image and select **Recognize text**. The Simplified and Traditional Chinese options also recognize English on the same image.
3. Check the result against the preview. Edit any mistakes, then copy the text or download a UTF-8 TXT file.

## Get clearer OCR results

Use a sharp, upright image with good contrast. If the image contains a lot of unrelated detail, crop to the text you need. Give names, numbers, and punctuation an extra check before relying on the result. The output is plain text, so tables and page layouts may need manual cleanup.

## Where is my image processed?

Recognition runs in your browser. The first run downloads the OCR engine and the selected language data. Your image and the extracted text stay on your device.
