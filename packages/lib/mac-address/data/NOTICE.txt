This snapshot contains public assignment records published by the IEEE
Registration Authority. IEEE is the authoritative source of these records.

Sources:
https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui/oui.csv
https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui28/mam.csv
https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui36/oui36.csv
https://standards-oui.ieee.org/iab/iab.csv
https://standards-oui.ieee.org/cid/cid.csv

Source URLs, retrieval date, SHA-256 checksums and record counts are recorded in
sources.json. The code license of this repository does not relicense IEEE's
registry data. IEEE's names and registry designations remain their respective
owners' marks.

The records identify registered assignees. They do not establish the manufacturer
of a particular device, its present owner, or its physical location. CID records
are company identifiers and do not allocate universally unique MAC addresses.
